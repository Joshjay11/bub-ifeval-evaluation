# Methodology

The completed pilot generated 96 responses: 12 cases, four conditions and two samples per case/condition. A was the Bub baseline with full Quintessence; B removed Quintessence; C substituted a plain-English semantic equivalent; D substituted generic high-quality assistant guidance. All other baseline components were held fixed. This was a section ablation; it did not remove every thematic reference elsewhere in the baseline.

All responses were generated by `zai-org/GLM-5.2` with temperature 0.72, top_p 0.92 and a 2,048-token output cap. Generation used a static system prompt and a case message, without memory, retrieval, tools or dynamic conversation context. The harness used non-streaming transport while Bub streamed. Sample numbers across conditions do not imply matched random seeds. The original D6 development history is provenance, not evidence of a causal model-tuning effect.

The correct description of the primary review is **human-primary blind review with AI-assisted rubric interpretation and rationale editing**. Numerical human ratings were selected by the human reviewer. AI was used for some rubric interpretation and rationale editing. The A/B/C/D mapping and model identities were hidden during human scoring. This should not be described as fully unaided human scoring. These role details are the experiment owner's clarification; the sealed numerical record remains unchanged.

The locked record contains 96 rating sets and zero pairwise preference records. Reviewer identity is null in that record; the number of distinct raters and inter-rater reliability are not documented there. The human review was locked before panel judging and reveal. Structured ratings are authoritative when prose is terse or absent. Blindness does not prevent a reviewer from inferring a condition from response style. The degree of AI influence on interpretation is not independently quantified by the locked scores.

The frozen composite is `sum(weight[d] * (score[d] - 1) / 4)`, on a 0-100 rubric-point scale. Average the two samples within each case/condition, then average the 12 equally weighted cases. Means of ordinal scores are the frozen reporting convention, not percentages of ability. The intervention gate stays separate from the composite. Pairwise wins/ties/losses compare case means; they are not human preference votes.

The frozen gate addressed unsupported-risk refusal, sanitization, redirection or strong warning. Human scoring broadened it to unsupported psychological interpretations that redirected the task or recast motives. This departure in interpretation is a methodology caveat. Preserve the recorded counts without silently relabeling them or presenting them as a validated refusal rate. B/C zero observed flags do not imply zero population risk; the B-C empirical [0,0] interval is degenerate and does not establish equivalence.

The secondary six-model panel evaluated the frozen anonymous responses with the rubric. The final six-model selection followed an earlier ten-model plan; excluded model records were preserved, and exclusions reflected interface incompatibility rather than measured response quality. Human and model results were analyzed separately and never pooled.

Seven judgments were missing due to terminal MAX_TOKENS failures after recovery was exhausted; none remained eligible to resume. Kimi's missing cells were Q08/A/sample 2, Q08/B/sample 2, Q08/C/sample 1 and Q08/D/sample 1. GLM's were Q02/A/sample 1, Q08/D/sample 1 and Q12/C/sample 2. Missing judgments were neither imputed nor scored as zero. Every judge/condition still covers all 12 cases, sometimes with only one sample. Missingness is concentrated in particular cases and may be nonrandom.

Judge-specific means average available samples within each case/condition, then equally weight covered cases. The panel consensus was a post-reveal descriptive summary: average available judges per candidate, then the two samples per case/condition, then the 12 cases. Each candidate has four to six valid judges. Its `n=24` per condition counts responses, not judgments. Actual judgment denominators are A 142, B 143, C 142 and D 142, each out of 144 expected. The JSON includes individual judge summaries, dimension means, case means, missing cells and intervention diagnostics.

GLM-5.2 is a same-family diagnostic because it shares the generator family. Removing it preserves the descriptive composite ordering. The six judges are not six independent experiments, and disagreement cannot be attributed to hidden motives. Across 78/96 responses, at least one dimension differed by two or more rating points among judges. The nine model gate fail votes use 569 returned judgments as their denominator; they must not be combined with seven human response flags or treated as nine distinct human-confirmed errors.

Cases are the meaningful experimental units. Exploratory 95% percentile bootstrap intervals use 20,000 whole-case resamples, seed 20261012, retaining the samples and judges inside each sampled case. Twelve selected clusters provide limited generalization. All six human composite intervals include zero. There was no frozen equivalence margin or materiality threshold, and multiple comparisons were not adjusted. Some secondary panel intervals exclude zero, but they do not replace the primary result or establish broad statistical significance.

The sealed reveal checked all 96 anonymous candidates against condition/sample assignments and response bytes. No experiment evidence was rewritten for this publication. The pre-reveal ledger seal remains a provenance anchor even though authorized reveal/reporting appended later ledger entries. The public JSON includes SHA-256 checksums of the protected report, results, locked review and input inventory, without publishing the path inventory or its contents. Hashes identify artifacts; they do not make the withheld prompts and responses independently reproducible.

For future runs, lock human numerical ratings separately from later AI rationale assistance, preserve both versions, and record assistance provenance. Freeze concrete gate examples and interpretation rules in advance. An independent gate-only review of the existing 40 responses in the five flagged cases is the smallest proposed follow-up; no such new review was performed for this publication.
